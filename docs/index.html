Note-worthy templates for people who take their cases seriously, and themselves only a little.

What is it?
TechNotes is a pocket-sized sidekick that turns a few quick answers into a clean, consistent case note. Pick a template, fill in the blanks, hit Copy, paste it into your ticket and move on to the next one. As close to a case-closed button as we could get.
What it does
  • Templates for every occasion. Cancellation, General Help, Change Request and Case Note come built in, plus any of your own from the + tab. Every case deserves a fitting note. No one-size-fits-all here.
  • Smart fields. Text boxes, dropdowns, toggle buttons and auto-calculated values that write the exact wording for you. Less typing, more winning.
  • Live preview. The note builds as you type, and anything left blank shows as _____ so you can spot it before you paste — unless it's marked Optional, in which case the whole line just stays out of the note. No blank left behind.
  • DNS records. Old and new DNS as tidy rows (type, host, value, TTL). Paste a zone file or a table from your DNS panel and get a change summary for free. Records that are on the record.
  • History. Snapshots of your setup are saved before changes, so you can always roll back from Settings. Rewind, without the tape.
  • infoHub. The infoHub button opens a separate, searchable library of your DNS settings, links, forms, requirements and canned replies, sorted into categories you can change. Entries support rich text formatting and images you can edit. Look it up, don't look it over.
  • Reference tables. Your cheat sheets, with one click to copy any cell. Copy-cat approved.
  • Search and reorder. Find a tab fast, or drag your tabs into your favorite order. Tab-ulate your way to the top.
  • Remembers your work. Close the window mid-note and everything is still there when you're back. It never leaves you hanging.
  • Backup and themes. Export and Import live in Settings, and so does the Dark / Light switch (under General). We won't judge if you go to the light side.
Quick tips
  • Press Alt+C to open it. Faster than "may I place you on a brief hold?"
  • Edit template changes the wording. Wrap any word in {curly braces} and it becomes a field on the form.
  • Clear fields, right under the tabs, wipes the current template for a fresh start. A clean slate, and no case of the Mondays.
  • Item not in a dropdown? Pick ✎ Type manually… at the bottom of the list and type it. It goes into this note only; press Add to list if you want it saved to the dropdown for next time.
  • Want a detail on the same line, only for certain issues (e.g. Issue: Activate domain. ABN: 12345)? Make a field, use Show this field to pick the issue, and set its Lead-in text to . ABN: .
  • Everything stays in this browser: no accounts, no servers, no cloud. Just you and your notes.
